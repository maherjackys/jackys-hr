# Dubai HR Policy Documents

This folder contains PDF files for **Dubai HR Policies** and **UAE Labor Law** regulations.

## How to Use

Place your Dubai HR policy PDF files in this directory. The HR Policy Assistant will automatically index them when the **Dubai HR Policy** source is selected.

## Supported Document Types

- Dubai Labour Law (Federal Law No. 33 of 2021)
- DIFC Employment Law
- Dubai Government HR Policies
- MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) regulations
- Any Dubai/UAE HR policy documents in PDF format

## Notes

- Supported format: **PDF only**
- Documents are indexed automatically on first use
- Switch between Company Policy and Dubai HR Policy using the source selector in the app
